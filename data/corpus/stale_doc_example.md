# Product Pricing Policy (Legacy — 2023)

> NOTE FOR DEMO MAINTAINERS: this document is intentionally outdated.
> It exists to trigger the stale-context failure mode in Moment 3 — the
> retriever should be able to find this document (it's topically relevant
> and well-embedded), but its content no longer reflects the "true" answer.
> Pair this with a fresher, correct document in the same corpus and a
> query that plausibly matches both, so contextual retrieval / recency

Our standard support plan includes email support only, with a response
time target of 48 hours. Priority phone support is available only on the
Enterprise tier. Refunds are issued within 30 days of purchase, at the
discretion of the billing team.
